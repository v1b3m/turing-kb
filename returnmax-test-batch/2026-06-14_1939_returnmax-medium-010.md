Three credit parts:

**Part 1 — Child Tax Credit: ✅ works.** `/deductions/child-tax-credit` is a gate ("Do you want us to check this credit?"). Selecting **Yes → Continue** advances to the result that lists **Ella Rivera** as a qualifying child with the **$2,000** credit assigned. Verified on screen. Screenshot `assets/medium-010-ctc.png`.

**Part 2 — Child & Dependent Care Credit (Form 2441): data correct, credit computed (stale seed).** The seed already contains the provider the prompt asks to "add": `credits.childDependentCare.providers[0]` = **Mission Kids After School Club**, TIN **81-2345678**, amountPaid **3600** (plus address). The engine computes the credit (`getChildDependentCareCreditTotal`, `lib/tax-engine.ts:301`) and it flows into total credits. So nothing to add — same stale-seed pattern as 001/002/004/007. (The multi-step `/deductions/child-care` wizard — gate → "uncommon situations" Yes/No → provider/amount → review — is navigable; I verified the underlying seeded data is correct rather than re-typing it.)

**Part 3 — Earned Income Credit: prompt unachievable; app shows a hardcoded "not qualified".** `/deductions/earned-income-credit` immediately renders **"It looks like you don't qualify"** — there is no eligibility wizard and no EIC-estimate path. Two distinct problems:

1. **Prompt is factually wrong for this filer.** Maya is HoH with AGI ≈ $90,240. The 2025 EIC income limit for HoH/1 child is ≈ $49,084, so she is **correctly not EIC-eligible**. The prompt's "complete the eligibility flow … confirming the EIC estimate populates in the header" cannot happen — a $90k filer has **$0 EIC** by law. So even a fully-correct app would not populate an EIC estimate here.
2. **App EIC flow is a not-qualified-only stub (latent bug).** `EarnedIncomeCreditFlow` (`app/index/tto/deductions/earned-income-credit/earned-income-credit-flow.tsx:13-18`) unconditionally renders `EarnedIncomeCreditNotQualifiedStep`, and the view type is literally `EarnedIncomeCreditView = "not-qualified"` (only one value; `lib/earned-income-credit-flow.ts:1`). There is **no income/filing-status/qualifying-children gating** despite prep_work claiming it. For Maya the "not qualified" output is *coincidentally correct*, but **any genuinely EIC-eligible filer (low income + qualifying child) would be wrongly told they don't qualify** — EIC can never be claimed in this app. See Bugs found.

**Verdict: bug-found.** Parts 1–2 work (CTC confirms Ella + $2,000; child-care provider data correct and credit computed). Part 3 cannot be completed: the app's EIC flow is a hardcoded "not-qualified" stub with no eligibility logic or estimate path, and separately the prompt's EIC expectation is factually impossible for a ~$90k filer. State restored to clean seed afterward.


## Bugs found

_One bullet per bug; keep `bug_count` in sync._

- **EIC eligibility is never evaluated — the Earned Income Credit flow always renders "It looks like you don't qualify".** `app/index/tto/deductions/earned-income-credit/earned-income-credit-flow.tsx:13-18` unconditionally renders `EarnedIncomeCreditNotQualifiedStep`; the flow view type is `"not-qualified"`-only (`lib/earned-income-credit-flow.ts:1`); `estimateEarnedIncomeCredit` returns 0 whenever `credits.earnedIncomeCredit` is null/not-applicable (`lib/tax-engine.ts:254`), and nothing in the flow ever sets it to applicable. Consequence: **no filer can ever qualify for or claim EIC**, and the prompt's "EIC estimate populates in the header" is impossible. Not triggered as a *wrong dollar amount* for Maya (she is genuinely ineligible at ~$90k AGI), but it is a real correctness gap for any eligible filer. **Fix deferred** — implementing real EIC eligibility (income thresholds by filing status + #children, earned-income phase-in/out, investment-income limit, estimate) is a feature build needing product scope, not a surgical patch; flagged, not implemented.
