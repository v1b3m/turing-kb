## Bugs found

_One bullet per bug; keep `bug_count` in sync._

- **SSN page shows the SSN in plain text — no masked input and no visibility toggle.** `/index/tto/my-info/ssn` renders `321-54-9876` as a plain `type="text"` input with no masking and no show/hide (eye) control. prep_work explicitly expects "a masked input and visibility toggle", and the prompt instructs "toggling the visibility to verify the format" — impossible as built. This is both a privacy concern (SSN, a sensitive identifier, is always visible — ironically beside a "We take your privacy seriously" link) and a task-blocker for the visibility sub-step. Suggested fix: render the SSN masked by default with a reveal toggle (mirrors the W-2/medium-001 pattern of exposing expected UI affordances). **Not implemented** (continue-testing mode).
- **(Secondary, tangential) Filing-status downgrade: a single filer with a qualifying dependent is set to "Single" instead of Head of Household.** The filing-status step (`/index/tto/my-info/filing-status`) derives status purely from marital status, ignoring `hasDependents`/`numberOfDependents`, and committing it overwrites the seed's correct `head_of_household` → `single`, reducing the refund ($10,966 → $9,276). Discovered while navigating the My Info wizard; not exercised by this task's intended actions. Belongs to a filing-status task. **Not implemented.**
