## Bugs found

_One bullet per bug; keep `bug_count` in sync._

- **[FIXED — PR #268] Medical & dental expenses flow never surfaces the 7.5%-of-AGI deductibility floor.** Entering qualifying medical expenses below the threshold (e.g. $500 vs the $6,768 floor at AGI $90,240) and finishing the flow showed only the generic "itemizing saves you the most" tip (`getItemizedDeductionTipMessage`) — **no 7.5% AGI threshold warning anywhere** (grep-confirmed across `app/`+`components/`+`lib/`). The deduction math *does* apply the floor (`computeItemizedDeduction`, `lib/tax-engine.ts:205` = `max(0, totalExpenses − AGI×0.075)`), but the filer was never told their expenses won't reduce their taxes — so the prompt's "confirm the 7.5% AGI threshold warning appears" was impossible. Fixed by adding a warning to the medical summary (`ThanksStep`) when the entered total is ≤ 7.5% of AGI (AGI from `computeFilingSummary`). Verified live: $500 → warning shown; $8,000 → no warning.
