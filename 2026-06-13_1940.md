The clone application does not display section-level progress indicators in the left navigation panel (My Info, Federal, State, Final Review, File), making it difficult for users to understand completion status throughout the filing process.

### **Steps to Reproduce:**

- Launch the TurboTax clone.
- Begin a tax filing workflow.
- Navigate through sections such as My Info, Federal, and State.
- Observe the navigation panel on the left side.

### **Actual Result:**
No visual progress indicator isn't  displayed for individual sections.

![[Pasted image 20260613194048.png]]

### **Expected Result:**
The application should mirror the behavior of the original TurboTax application:

- Incomplete sections should display a gray circular progress indicator.
- Sections in progress should show a partially completed green progress ring.
- Completed sections should display a green circle containing a white checkmark.
