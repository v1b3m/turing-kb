When accessing the Income section, the clone application skips the income category selection experience available in the original TurboTax application and immediately navigates to the Income & Expenses page.

### **Steps to Reproduce:**

- Open the TurboTax clone.
- Navigate to the Income section.
- Click the Income menu item.

### **Actual Result:**
The application immediately opens the "Your Income and Expenses" page.

![[Pasted image 20260614080426.png]]

### **Expected Result:**
The application should first display an expandable list of income categories similar to the original TurboTax experience, including:

- Wages & Salaries (W-2)
- Self-Employment Income
- Unemployment & Paid Family Leave
- Refunds from State and Local Tax Returns
- Interest and Dividends
- Investments and Savings
- Retirement Plans and Social Security
- Other applicable income categories

Selecting a category should then navigate to the corresponding workflow.
