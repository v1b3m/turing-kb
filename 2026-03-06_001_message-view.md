### Problem Statement

Email detail currently works functionally but looks under-styled relative to other record views. We need parity with established detail-page styling and component patterns while keeping current routing/data behavior.

### In Scope

- Email message view page UI structure and styling.
- Reuse of shared components/styles from existing detail pages.
- Consistent field rendering for email-specific fields from store data.
- Preserve navigation back to parent case/list behavior.

### Out Of Scope

- Changing email data model/store contracts.
- Reworking compose popup behavior.
- Adding backend/API integration.
- Broad refactor of global headers/layout.

- Style drift risk if we mix `CasePageHeader` and `DetailPageHeader` patterns inconsistently.
- Potential responsive regression for long email body/attachments.
- Route/state coupling risk if field mapping changes while restyling.
- Existing hardcoded colors in form/case components may conflict with tokenized styling expectations.

### Options

1. Minimal reskin in `EmailDetailPage` only.
Pros: fastest change, low blast radius.
Cons: may still diverge from established detail-page structure.

Pros: long-term consistency.
Cons: larger scope than this task and higher regression risk.
